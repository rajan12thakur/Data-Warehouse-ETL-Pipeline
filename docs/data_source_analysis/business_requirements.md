Before writing an ingestion script, we need to understand:

What is the source?
What data does it contain?
Who owns it?
What business process does it support?
What tables/files exist?
What do the columns mean?
How is the data extracted?
How much data is there?
How frequently does it change?


2. Decide technical questions:
What technology is used?
Where is the source?
Can we use an API?
Can we extract files?
Can we connect directly to the database?
Is the load full or incremental?
How much data is expected?
What authentication is required?
